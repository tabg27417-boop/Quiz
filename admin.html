Newsfeed Preview — commonly missed MCQs from all attempts

This newsfeed is publicly visible on the student dashboard. It shows MCQs that students most commonly get wrong, sorted by error count. Adding explanations to questions makes them more educational in the feed.

How the Newsfeed Works

  • Scans all student attempt submissions from Firestore
  • Finds MCQs where students answered incorrectly
  • Ranks them by how many students got each one wrong
  • Shows question text, explanation, subject badge, and error count
  • Auto-scrolls as a ticker — hovering pauses it
  • Visible to all logged-in students on their dashboard
